Auditor — voluntary disclosure

Alice chooses to disclose exactly one claim: its nullifier and a salt. The auditor recomputes the audit fingerprint from that pair and checks the nullifier is on the public ledger. Nothing else is revealed — not the wallet, not the secret key, not which commitment claimed.

Alice must claim first, then disclose.
